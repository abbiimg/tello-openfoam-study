Both layouts remain credible. Use the square as a provisional working reference because its sampled means are consistently higher; do not permanently eliminate the circle. At these tested points, inward 10 degrees increases fine-grid mean lift by 1.1905% for the square and 1.2569% for the circle. Both fine pairs pass the finite late-window screens, but the gains decrease from medium to fine grids. This stage does not identify an optimal angle or establish grid-independent gain. The narrow medium-circle drift failure remains visible in the data rather than being discarded.

These are transient OpenFOAM actuator-volume results with 7.62 cm propeller-sized regions, a 19.2923 W total modeled electrical budget and documented assumed power losses. The model shares one interacting flow field, but does not resolve blades, synchronization phase, common-RPM fixed-pitch operation, airframe forces or measured motor performance. The figures therefore describe the stated model, not measured Tello lift or a global optimum. Rotor-count comparison and the wider angle/spacing stage follow this layout report.
